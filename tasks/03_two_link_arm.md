# Task 03 — Questions about a 2-joint robot arm

**Status:** Not started in this repository. **No solutions or code included.**

## Prerequisites to study first

- Circle and coordinate representation
- Sine/cosine and angle conventions
- Vectors and derivatives

## Learning questions

- What information would be needed to describe where the tip of a two-joint arm is?
- How does changing only one joint affect the tip?
- What might a derivative tell us about its motion?
- When could the arm's possible movements become limited?

## Future application questions

- **Robotics:** How could this idea lead to forward and inverse kinematics, then control?
- **Physics:** What extra physical concepts become necessary when the arm has mass and motors?
- **AI:** How could optimization help an arm approach a target?
